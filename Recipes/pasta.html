Here is a guide to make Pasta oki

Ingredients Required:

  1. 1 (16 ounce) package pasta
  2. 2 tablespoons olive oil
  3. ½ cup chopped onion
  4. 2 ½ tablespoons pesto or more to taste
  5. salt to taste
  6. freshly ground black pepper to taste
  7. 2 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese

*Directions:

Step 1.

Gather all ingredients.

Step 2.

Fill a large pot with lightly salted water and bring to a rolling boil. Stir in pasta and return to a boil. Cook pasta uncovered, stirring occasionally, until tender yet firm to the bite, 8 to 10 minutes. Drain; transfer into a large bowl.

Step 3.

Meanwhile, heat oil in a skillet over medium-low heat. Add onion; cook and stir until softened, about 3 minutes.

Step 4.

Stir in pesto, salt, and black pepper until warmed through.

Step 5.

Stir pesto mixture into hot pasta; stir in grated cheese to coat.
